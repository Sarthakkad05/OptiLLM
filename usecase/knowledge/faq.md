# ShopEasy — Customer Support FAQ

**Company:** ShopEasy — Online Retail Platform
**Support contact:** support@shopeasy.example.com

---

## Orders

**Q: How do I track my order?**
Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking email with a link. You can also visit shopeasy.example.com/orders and enter your order ID. Tracking updates every 4–6 hours.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order enters fulfillment and cannot be cancelled. Contact support immediately if you need to cancel.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after ordering?**
Address changes are possible within 30 minutes of placing an order. After that, the shipment is locked. Email support@shopeasy.example.com with your order ID and new address.

**Q: My order says delivered but I haven't received it.**
Wait 24 hours — deliveries sometimes scan early. If the package still hasn't arrived, file a missing package report at shopeasy.example.com/claims or email support.

**Q: Can I combine two orders into one shipment?**
Orders can be combined if they haven't entered processing yet. Contact support with both order IDs within 30 minutes.

## Returns & Refunds

**Q: What is your return policy?**
We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy on all items. Items must be in their original condition with tags attached. Electronics must be unopened or have a manufacturing defect.

**Q: How do I start a return?**
Go to shopeasy.example.com/returns, enter your order ID and email, select the items you want to return, and print the prepaid return label. Drop off the package at any USPS or UPS location.

**Q: How long does a refund take?**
Once we receive your return, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. The amount will appear on your original payment method within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

**Q: Do I have to pay return shipping?**
Return shipping is free for all orders. We provide a prepaid label. For international returns, a $15 fee is deducted from the refund.

**Q: Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?**
Yes. On the returns page, select "Exchange" instead of "Return" and choose your replacement item. Stock permitting, the exchange ships within 24 hours of us receiving your return.

**Q: What items cannot be returned?**
Final sale items, digital downloads, gift cards, and perishable goods cannot be returned. Swimwear and underwear require original hygiene seal to be intact.

## Payments & Pricing

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and ShopEasy Gift Cards. Cryptocurrency is not accepted.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
Yes. We use 256-bit SSL encryption and are PCI DSS Level 1 compliant. We never store full card numbers — only the last 4 digits for reference.

**Q: Do you offer price matching?**
Yes. If you find the same item at a lower price on Amazon, Walmart, or Target within 7 days of purchase, we'll match it. Email support with a link to the competitor listing.

**Q: Can I use multiple discount codes?**
Only one discount code can be applied per order. Discount codes cannot be combined with flash sale prices.

**Q: Do prices include tax?**
Prices shown exclude tax. Tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address and the applicable state/local rates.

## Shipping

**Q: What are your shipping options?**
- Standard (5–7 business days): Free on orders over $35
- Express (2–3 business days): $8.99
- Overnight (next business day): $19.99
- International: Starting at $24.99, 7–21 business days

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
Yes. We ship to over 80 countries. Customs duties and import taxes are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in the ShopEasy price.

**Q: When will my order ship?**
Orders placed before 2 PM EST Monday–Friday ship the same day. Orders after 2 PM ship the next business day. Orders on weekends ship Monday.

## Account & Membership

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
Go to shopeasy.example.com/login, click "Forgot Password," and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within 2 minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

**Q: What is ShopEasy Plus?**
ShopEasy Plus is our premium membership at $9.99/month. Benefits include free express shipping on all orders, 10% cashback on every purchase, early access to sales, and priority customer support.

**Q: How do I cancel my ShopEasy Plus subscription?**
Go to Account Settings → Subscription → Cancel. Your membership benefits continue until the end of the current billing period.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
Email privacy@shopeasy.example.com with subject "Account Deletion Request." Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days per GDPR/CCPA requirements.

## Technical Issues

**Q: The website won't load. What should I do?**
Try clearing your browser cache and cookies, then reload. If the issue persists, try a different browser or device. Check status.shopeasy.example.com for any ongoing service incidents.

**Q: My discount code isn't working.**
Ensure the code is entered exactly as received (codes are case-sensitive). Check the code expiry date. If the issue persists, contact support with the code and your cart details.
